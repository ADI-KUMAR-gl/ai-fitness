The Problem

Critical Flaws In Current Fitness Technology

1. Manual Set Logging Fatigue

Trainees are forced to manually type reps and weights between exhausting sets, leading to high friction, inaccurate workout logs, and abandoned tracking apps.

2. Camera Gym Crowd Occlusion

Existing computer vision apps only work in solitary living rooms. In busy commercial gyms, people walking past corrupt pose estimation and cause false rep counts.

3. Catastrophic 1RM Injury Risk

Traditional programs mandate lifting to absolute physical failure to measure 1-Rep Max, exposing lifters to severe spinal shear stress and acute muscle tears.

4. Hardware Ecosystem Silos

Wearables (Apple Watch, Polar, Garmin) trap cardiac data inside closed ecosystems, incapable of syncing live with video biomechanics in the browser.

Research Deliverable 1

Global Literature Review

Computer Vision Pose

Google MediaPipe, OpenPose, AlphaPose: Landmark models for 2D/3D skeletal keypoints. However, existing implementations lack spatial Re-ID anchors and fail under dynamic gym occlusion.

Linear Transducers

GymAware, Vitruve, Tendo Units: Athletic labs use \$2,000+ tethered hardware to calculate barbell speed. Highly accurate, but financially inaccessible to standard gymgoers.

VBT Clinical Science

Dr. González-Badillo, Dr. Bryan Mann: Proved that concentric velocity ($m/s$) has an extraordinary linear correlation ($R^2 > 0.98$) with % of 1RM, enabling submaximal math modeling.

Research Deliverable 2 & 3

Proposed Solution & Mathematical Modeling

A. Single-Athlete Optical Lock

Centroid Re-ID anchors to foreground athlete ($A_1$). Peripheral subjects ($B_k$) with Intersection over Union ($IoU$) < 0.20 are filtered automatically:

IoU(A_1, B_k) = \frac{\text{Area}(A_1 \cap B_k)}{\text{Area}(A_1 \cup B_k)} < 0.20 \implies \text{FILTERED}

B. Submaximal 1RM Regression

Least-squares linear regression profile fits warmup velocities and extrapolates true 1RM at terminal Minimum Velocity Threshold (MVT):

v = m \cdot L + c \quad \implies \quad \text{e1RM} = \frac{\text{MVT} - c}{m}
● Zero maximal lifts to failure required
