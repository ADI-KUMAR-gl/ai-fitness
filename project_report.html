1. Research-Based Deliverables

1.1 Global Literature Review

Over the past decade, sports biomechanics and computer vision have progressed along distinct, fragmented vectors:

  • Human Pose Estimation Models (Google MediaPipe, OpenPose, AlphaPose): While Deep Neural Networks have enabled robust 2D/3D skeletal keypoint localization in controlled environments, existing implementations assume a single isolated subject or collapse when visual occlusion occurs in dynamic settings.
  • Linear Position Transducers & Accelerometers (GymAware, Vitruve, Tendo Units): Athletic laboratories rely on \$2,000+ tethered hardware transducers to measure mean concentric barbell velocity ($m/s$). These are physically cumbersome, expensive, and unavailable to normal trainees.
  • Commercial Cardiac Telemetry (Whoop, Apple HealthKit, Polar Flow): Wearables monitor heart rate and Heart Rate Variability (HRV), but store this data within closed, proprietary mobile silos that cannot natively communicate with real-time gym vision feeds.
  • Velocity-Based Training (VBT) Clinical Foundation (González-Badillo et al., 2017; Dr. Bryan Mann): Exercise physiology has demonstrated that barbell concentric velocity has an extraordinarily tight linear correlation ($R^2 > 0.98$) with relative percentage of 1RM, establishing that 1RM can be mathematically modeled submaximally.

1.2 Identification of Critical Research Gaps

Identified Research Gaps in Existing Fitness Technology:

Gap 1 (Crowd Occlusion): Multi-person tracking confusion in commercial gyms where background gymgoers corrupt kinematic calculations.
Gap 2 (Catastrophic 1RM Injury Risk): Traditional strength programs mandate lifting to physical failure, generating excessive spinal shear stress.
Gap 3 (Hardware Data Silos): Inability to capture live raw ECG/PPG cardiac streams directly inside web browsers without proprietary native SDKs.
Gap 4 (Static Periodization): Rigid 12-week static spreadsheets ignoring autonomic central nervous system (CNS) fatigue.

1.3 Proposed Solution & Mathematical Model

APEX synthesizes computer vision with least-squares mathematical modeling and Web Bluetooth GATT streams:

A. Single-Athlete Optical Re-ID & IoU Crowd Rejection

The camera engine establishes an optical centroid anchor on the primary foreground athlete ($A_1$). For any peripheral subject ($B_k$), the engine computes the Intersection over Union ($IoU$) against the focal tracking anchor:

IoU(A_1, B_k) = \frac{\text{Area}(A_1 \cap B_k)}{\text{Area}(A_1 \cup B_k)} \quad \implies \quad \text{If } IoU < 0.20 \implies \text{FILTER / DISMISS BYSTANDER}

B. Submaximal 1RM Linear Regression Equation

During submaximal warm-up sets (3–4 sets between 50%–80% load), the system computes the least-squares linear velocity profile:

v = m \cdot L + c \quad \text{where } m = \frac{N\sum(xy) - \sum x \sum y}{N\sum x^2 - (\sum x)^2}

At the calibrated Minimum Velocity Threshold (MVT) (Squat: $0.18\text{ m/s}$, Bench: $0.15\text{ m/s}$, Deadlift: $0.12\text{ m/s}$), true 1RM is solved directly without lifting to failure:

\text{e1RM} = \frac{\text{MVT} - c}{m} \quad \text{with Goodness-of-Fit } R^2 = 1 - \frac{SS_{\text{res}}}{SS_{\text{tot}}} \ge 0.99

3.2 Examiner Viva / Defense Q&A Preparation

Q1: How does your camera model filter out bystanders in a crowded gym?

Defense Answer: "Sir, we implement Spatial Centroid Re-ID combined with Bounding Box Scale Analysis. The primary athlete occupies the foreground center with the dominant bounding area. Peripheral subjects exhibit an Intersection over Union ($IoU$) < 0.20 and high centroid variance. The engine classifies them as crowd bystanders, discarding their keypoint vectors from rep and velocity math."

Q2: How accurate is 1RM prediction without lifting heavy weights to failure?

Defense Answer: "Sir, sports science (Dr. González-Badillo) has proven that concentric barbell velocity has an exceptionally tight linear relationship ($R^2 > 0.98$) with load percentage. By performing 3–4 submaximal warmup sets, our system computes the linear equation $v = m \cdot L + c$. Extrapolating to the exercise's terminal Minimum Velocity Threshold (MVT 0.18 m/s for squat) predicts true 1RM with clinical precision while eliminating spinal injury risk."

Q3: Why use Web Bluetooth API instead of a mobile app?

Defense Answer: "Sir, native mobile apps fragment data into closed silos (Apple Health, Garmin Connect). The Web Bluetooth API (`navigator.bluetooth`) allows zero-install, zero-friction browser pairing directly to the standard GATT Heart Rate Service (`0x180D`), streaming raw RR-intervals into our clinical canvas oscilloscope with point-to-point hardware encryption."
